**Assigned Collaborator:** Friend 3  
**Feature Branch:** `feature/audio-studio`  

---

## 🎯 Task Objectives

Independent indie game developers, filmmakers, and podcast producers lack real-time adaptive soundscapes tailored to dynamic emotional pacing.

### Seed Prompting Inspirations to Implement:
1. **Adaptive Stems:** Dynamically shift musical stem arrangements based on visual scene tension.
2. **Zero-Shot Foley:** Synthesize zero-shot cinematic Foley sound effects from text cues and action descriptions.
3. **Micro-Tonal Harmonies:** AI-driven vocal harmony arrangement matching micro-tonal scales and pitch adjustments.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & APIs
- **Web Audio API** (Real-time dynamic stem mixing and sound nodes)
- **Lyria / MusicLM Models** (Generative audio score synthesis)
- **Audio Spectrogram Embeddings** (Tension audio energy classification)
- **Realtime MIDI Streams** (Dynamic note trigger and pitch-bend modulation)

---

## 📁 Files in this Module
- `engine.py`: Dynamic stem arrangement, text-to-Foley synthesis, and micro-tonal harmony tuner.
- `test_audio.py`: Unit tests validating tension-to-stem mapping and Foley generation.
